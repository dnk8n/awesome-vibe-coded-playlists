# Bump — The Complete Series (RPM, South Africa)

Twice a year from 1997, South Africa's RPM label put out **Bump** — the country's
definitive commercial dance compilation, mixed by **DJ Costa**. This document walks all
35 volumes and, for every track, hunts the **exact mix as quoted on the Bump sleeve**
back to its original home on Discogs: a **single, EP or full-length album — never
another compilation** (the master release is linked only when its own tracklist quotes
that exact mix). Artist and track are copied **verbatim from the Bump release page**;
Genre, Style and Released come from the page actually linked (a release shows its full
date where Discogs has one; a master shows the year). Every YouTube link is validated,
preferring uploads titled with the exact quoted mix; the Contents table opens each
volume as an anonymous YouTube playlist in sleeve order.

**Legend**

- **ID** — `series.disc.track`, two digits each: `01.01.01` is Bump 1, disc 1, track 1.
- **SA** — ✓ = Confirmed South African artist (the credited artist's Discogs profile
  names South Africa or a South African city; blank means unconfirmed, never guessed).
- **Discogs** — the page linked: `Master` only when the master tracklist quotes the
  exact mix, else the specific `Single`/`EP`/`Album`/`12"` page (a `*` marks an
  unofficial pressing).
- **Status** — two marks per row: the Discogs hunt (**✅** exact quoted mix found on a
  single/EP/album · **❌** not found there — see Notes), then the YouTube link
  (**🎯** upload titled with the exact quoted mix · **▶** fallback upload, see Notes ·
  **∅** nothing usable found).
- Mix names are matched with sleeve-tolerance: generic words (*mix, remix, version,
  12"*) are interchangeable; a *vocal*-variant match is accepted only when no stricter
  pressing exists. Distinctive words (a remixer's name, *dub, edit, bootleg…*) must
  match exactly.
