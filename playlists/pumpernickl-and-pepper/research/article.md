# Pumpernickl & Pepper — Dark Loaf, Hot Spice

**A two-hour back-to-back festival set. Forty records, two decks, one plate.**

## The name did the curating

*Pumpernickel* is the darkest bread there is: whole rye, no shortcuts, steamed sixteen hours at a low
heat until it comes out near-black — dense, sour, faintly sweet, built to keep. *Pepper* is the
opposite instinct entirely: heat that arrives now, sharp and bright, and makes people sneeze. Put
the two on one plate and you get something hearty and slightly rude. Comfort with a kick.

That is the set.

**🍞 Pumpernickl plays the loaf** — dark, slow-fermented, low and dense. Berlin dub techno with the
tape hiss left in, Detroit at its most patient, records that reward standing still and letting the
bass do the work.

**🌶 Pepper plays the spice** — hot, percussive, immediate. Congas, 303s, gqom drums, machine funk,
acid that stings rather than soothes.

Neither of you plays a whole hour. The set alternates deck by deck — that column is the running
order — so the floor keeps getting hit from two directions. Three records are marked **🍞🌶**: both
hands on the mixer, agreed in advance.

## How to read it

- **Deck** — whose record it is. Follow it top to bottom and the b2b runs itself.
- **≈BPM** — a feel estimate for sequencing, not a measured value. Trust your player.
- **Find it** — a hard Discogs link for every row (the master page, so you can dig to any pressing
  via *See all versions*) plus a YouTube link taken from that same Discogs page's community-attached
  videos, not from open search.
- **Artist and Track** are copied character-for-character from the linked Discogs page, mix names
  and all. Where that differs from how the record is usually spoken about, the italic line in the
  Notes says so.
- **The bench** at the bottom holds six verified swaps, one per pressure point, with the same links.

Two hours over forty records is three minutes a piece, which no one actually plays. Treat the last
few in each course as surplus: drop them when the floor is running hot, and the arc still lands.

## The five courses

**I. The Starter** · 20:00 → 20:20 · *the culture is alive but nothing has risen yet* — dub, deep,
hissy, warm. Nobody is being asked to do anything except arrive.

**II. Proving** · 20:20 → 20:45 · *dough under pressure* — percussion arrives. Tribal, Afro, Latin,
gqom. Still house tempo, but it is now moving without you.

**III. The Grinder** · 20:45 → 21:10 · *spice into the mill* — Chicago acid, machine funk, electro.
The 303s here are the mean ones, which is the point: this is the dark half of acid, not the smiley
half.

**IV. Crust** · 21:10 → 21:45 · *maximum heat, outside goes black* — the peak. Twelve records, and
they are meant to hurt a bit.

**V. The Last Slice** · 21:45 → 22:00 · *what you eat standing at the counter* — the gear change
down. Latin strings, Detroit strings, gospel. Send them home fed.
